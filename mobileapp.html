Research Project Mobile App

This mobile app page represents the storage comparison app created for the project. The purpose of the app is to help users compare Solid-State Drives and Hard Disk Drives by reviewing speed, durability, cost, and storage capacity. The app connects to the research topic because it gives users a quick way to decide which storage option fits their needs. For example, a student may choose an SSD for faster performance, while a user needing large backup storage may choose an HDD. This page is included as the mobile app link for the final content page.
