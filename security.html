Security and Social Concerns

Both Hard Disk Drives and Solid-State Drives have security risks. Hard Disk Drives can fail when their moving parts are damaged, which may cause data loss if users do not have backups. Solid-State Drives are more durable, but they also have limits because they can only write data a certain number of times before wearing out over time (Bhushan, 2023).

Another concern is unauthorized access. If someone gains access to a storage device, they may be able to steal personal or business information. This is especially important for companies that store customer records, financial information, or other sensitive data. Users can reduce these risks by using passwords, encryption, and regular backups (Dipert, 2009).

Storage technology also creates social concerns because people depend heavily on digital files for school, work, communication, and entertainment. When data is lost or stolen, it can affect people’s daily lives. For this reason, users must understand how to protect their storage devices and keep backup copies of important information.
