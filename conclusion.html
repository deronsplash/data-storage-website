Future and Conclusion

Future research should focus on improving the endurance, efficiency, and sustainability of Solid-State Drives. Advances in storage technology may lead to solutions that combine the large storage capacity of HDDs with the speed and reliability of SSDs (Skendzic, Kovacic, & Tijan, 2016).

In conclusion, the evolution from Hard Disk Drives to Solid-State Drives shows major progress in computing technology. SSDs offer improved speed, durability, and performance, while HDDs remain useful for cost-effective storage. Understanding the differences between these technologies helps users make informed decisions based on their needs. As technology continues to advance, data storage will remain an important part of modern computing systems.
