Research Project Blog

This blog page summarizes the main idea of the research project in a shorter format. The topic explains how Solid-State Drives and Hard Disk Drives are both used for data storage, but they work differently. SSDs are generally faster and more durable because they do not use moving parts, while HDDs are often cheaper for large amounts of storage. The blog supports the website by giving readers a simple overview before they read the full research pages. It is connected to the project because it explains why storage technology matters for everyday computer users.
