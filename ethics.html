Legal and Ethical Issues

The switch from Hard Disk Drives to Solid-State Drives raises legal and ethical concerns. One major issue is electronic waste. When people replace old storage devices, those devices may be thrown away. If they are not disposed of properly, they can harm the environment over time (Shin & Kim, 2021).

Another concern is data privacy. Storage devices often contain personal information, school files, business records, passwords, and other private data. If a device is sold, recycled, or thrown away without being erased correctly, someone else may be able to access that information. This can lead to identity theft, privacy violations, or misuse of private data (Mearian, 2024).

Because of these risks, people and organizations should erase data properly before getting rid of a storage device. They should also follow responsible recycling practices and data protection policies. These steps help protect both personal privacy and the environment.
